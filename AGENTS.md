## Communication Preferences

English is my preferred working language, but I am
still developing my technical English proficiency.

When discussing architecture or complex design decisions:

- Use clear, precise technical English.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon and overly elaborate prose.
- Explain unfamiliar terminology when it matters.
- Do not interpret my brief responses as approval.
- If my requirements are ambiguous, ask for clarification.
- If I express an idea imprecisely, help me formulate
  it more accurately without changing its meaning.
- If I use Chinese to explain a technical concern,
  respond with an accurate English formulation.
- Present important trade-offs explicitly.
- Keep proposals concise and focused on one decision
  at a time.

Most importantly:

Never proceed with architectural changes merely
because I have not objected.

Explicit approval is required before implementation.
